Her expression. She has made up her mind and boarded this night ride alone: a little melancholy, but resolved.
- eyes steady, clear and fixed on the distance; not dreamy, not sleepy, not looking down
- thin eyebrows drawn very slightly together and level, the quiet tension of someone holding to a decision
- mouth closed in a short, firm, straight line; no smile, no pout
- the sadness is only a trace, in the eyes. No tears, no frown, no anger, no blush

How she is drawn. This is the most important part: she must look as if the painter of Image 1 painted her in the same sitting. Match Image 3 point by point.
- hair: a few large flat colour shapes with only a handful of long strand lines, as in Image 3. No fine strand hatching, no dense parallel hair lines, no glossy hair highlights
- eyes: large, tall oval irises filled with one flat colour (muted green here), one small white highlight each, a thick dark upper lash line, no lower lashes, no gradient inside the iris, no second highlight

The earbud and its cable are the lightest thing on her, an off-white that still sits inside the night palette, drawn as a simple flat shape and one thin line
- her hair reads as chestnut brown under this light, darker at the top of the head where the cabin is dark
